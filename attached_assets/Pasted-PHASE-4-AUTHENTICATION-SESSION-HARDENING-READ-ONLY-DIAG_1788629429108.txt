==================================================

Perform a complete READ-ONLY security and reliability diagnostic of:

PHASE 4 — AUTHENTICATION & SESSION HARDENING

The previous system audit identified several potentially serious authentication/session issues:

1. Selected role can be lost or disagree with `/auth/me`.
2. JWTs are not revoked on logout.
3. Password changes may not terminate existing sessions.
4. Some family/child APIs may have broader staff authorization scopes than intended.
5. MFA/security-setting changes may lack adequate step-up authentication.
6. WhatsApp OTP authentication path may be inconsistent.
7. The general `/api/events` SSE stream may remain alive for part of its permitted lifetime after logout or role changes.

These were identified before Tasks #74 and #75.

Your first responsibility is to determine:

- Which findings are still true.
- Which were already fixed incidentally.
- Which are false positives.
- Which are architectural limitations rather than vulnerabilities.
- Which are HIGH/CRITICAL.
- Which require product/policy decisions.
- Which are the smallest safe fixes.
