PHASE 5 — SERVICE DATE & SERVICE-SLOT CORRECTNESS
READ-ONLY DIAGNOSTIC — DO NOT MODIFY CODE, DATABASE, SCHEMA, DATA, OR CONFIGURATION

You are now working on Phase 5 of the Shepherd Connect reliability/security hardening program.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT CONTEXT

Shepherd Connect is NOT a conventional school-management system.

Do NOT introduce or assume concepts such as:
- school day
- academic day
- business date
- school calendar day
- class day

The product currently operates around Sunday services.

The correct domain concepts for this phase are:

1. SERVICE DATE
   The calendar date representing the Sunday/service occurrence.

2. SERVICE SLOT
   The specific service occurrence within that date.

Current known service slots are:
- First Service
- Second Service

A child may legitimately attend First Service and Second Service on the same Service Date.

Therefore:

"same child + same calendar date" MUST NOT automatically mean duplicate attendance.

The Service Slot is part of the attendance identity/business meaning.

KNOWN HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

The previous system audit found:
- 2 child-days with multiple active attendance rows for different service slots.
- 0 exact duplicate attendance tuple groups.
- 0 picked-up custody records retaining active attendance.
- 0 conflicting custody groups.

This means multiple attendance records for the same child/date may be legitimate when they belong to different service slots.

Do not "fix" or merge those records merely because they share a calendar date.
